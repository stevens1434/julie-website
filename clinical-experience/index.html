Bedside Nursing Arc

Career experiences that shape Julie's advanced practice approach

Current pediatric nephrology practice

Julie currently serves in pediatric dialysis at University of Michigan C.S. Mott Children's Hospital, caring for acute and outpatient dialysis patients in one of only three pediatric dialysis units in Michigan.

THE PORTFOLIO AWAKENS

It is a period of clinical transition. After years of high-acuity bedside service, Julie Stevens prepares to move from critical care and specialty nursing into family nurse practitioner practice.

Carrying experience forged in pediatric nephrology, cardiac intensive care, PICU, travel nursing, and community-based advanced practice training, she now advances toward a broader mission of prevention, continuity, and equitable care.

Employers who enter this portfolio will discover leadership, scholarship, and clinical depth assembled into one hiring-ready dossier, prepared for the next frontier in family-centered practice.
